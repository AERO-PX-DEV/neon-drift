Put your own audio files here (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, flac). Subfolders are fine.

They are picked up automatically when you run `npm run dev` (restart it after adding files).
Name files "Artist - Title.mp3" for the best automatic matching to the
"Memory Reboot" playlist and the radio stations.

You can also drag files or whole folders onto the game window instead; those are
stored in your browser (IndexedDB) and survive reloads.
